# Draft Data Processing Agreement (Verwerkersovereenkomst)

**Status:** Draft for counsel — not executable. Align with GDPR Art. 28(3) and (optionally) Commission C2P SCCs (EU) 2021/915.

**Parties (placeholders)**

- **Controller:** the customer law firm / organisation using Qompose (“Customer”)
- **Processor:** [LEGAL ENTITY NAME] trading as Qompose (“Processor”)

## 1. Subject-matter and duration

Processor provides the Qompose SaaS platform: workspace tenancy, client and dossier management, document requests, file storage, questionnaires, client portal access, notifications, optional AI-assisted processing (e.g. transcription / document assistance), and related support.

Duration: term of the master subscription / Terms of Service, plus the data return or deletion period in section 7.

## 2. Nature and purpose of processing

Nature: collection, storage, organisation, retrieval, transmission, pseudonymisation/anonymisation where configured, deletion, and AI inference on Customer content where Customer enables those features.

Purpose: solely to provide the Services on documented instructions of the Customer.

## 3. Types of personal data

May include (depending on Customer use): identity and contact data; account credentials; professional role; client/matter identifiers; documents and questionnaire answers (which may contain special-category data if Customer uploads them); portal device fingerprints; IP addresses and security logs; audio recordings and derived transcripts; support correspondence.

## 4. Categories of data subjects

Customer staff users; Customer’s clients and other individuals whose data Customer enters or collects via the portal; Processor support personnel interacting with Customer tickets (limited).

## 5. Controller instructions and rights

Processor processes only on documented instructions of Customer (including configuration within the product and written instructions), unless Union or Member State law requires otherwise. Customer is responsible for the lawfulness of instructions and for informing its data subjects.

## 6. Processor obligations (Art. 28(3)(a)–(h))

(a) Documented instructions only, including regarding transfers.  
(b) Confidentiality commitments for authorised persons.  
(c) Appropriate Art. 32 security measures (see Product security documentation and Customer DPA schedule).  
(d) Sub-processors: general written authorisation with prior notice of changes and Customer right to object; cascade Art. 28(4) terms.  
(e) Assist with data subject requests (export/erasure tooling where available).  
(f) Assist with Arts. 32–36 (security, breach, DPIA) taking into account nature of processing.  
(g) Delete or return personal data after end of services, at Customer choice, and delete copies unless law requires retention.  
(h) Make information available for compliance and allow audits/inspections under agreed procedures.

## 7. Return and deletion

On termination: Customer may export data via product features where available. Processor deletes or anonymises Customer personal data within an agreed period after termination, except data Processor must retain as independent controller (e.g. billing) or under legal obligation.

## 8. International transfers

If processing or sub-processing occurs outside the EEA/UK, Processor ensures an adequacy decision, SCCs, or other Art. 46 safeguard. Details: [TRANSFER SCHEDULE — counsel].

## 9. Sub-processors

Initial list: [HOSTING / OBJECT STORAGE / EMAIL / AI PROVIDER / MONITORING — counsel]. Updates notified per section 6(d).

## 10. Breach notification

Processor notifies Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting Customer data, with information reasonably required for Customer’s Art. 33/34 duties.

## 11. Liability and precedence

Commercial liability caps and indemnities: as in the master Terms (counsel). This DPA prevails over conflicting privacy terms in order forms regarding Art. 28 matters.
