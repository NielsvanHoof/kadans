# Cookie statement

**Draft — pending legal review.**

**Last updated:** 11 August 2026

Qompose uses cookies and similar storage on this domain. This statement explains what we use and why. Full technical inventory: maintained internally for counsel (`docs/legal/cookie-inventory.md`).

## What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We also use related browser storage (for example `localStorage`) for the same preference purposes.

## How we use cookies

### Strictly necessary

These are required to provide the service you request (login, security, session). They do not require consent under the ePrivacy “strictly necessary” exemption.

| Cookie            | Duration                                      | Purpose                                   |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| `qompose-session` | About 120 minutes of inactivity               | Keeps you signed in / maintains session   |
| `XSRF-TOKEN`      | Session                                       | Protects forms and API calls against CSRF |
| `remember_web_*`  | Persistent (only if you choose “Remember me”) | Keeps staff login across browser restarts |

### Functionality / preferences

These remember choices you make (language, theme, sidebar). We set them to make the interface usable across visits. **We do not currently use advertising or analytics cookies.**

| Cookie          | Duration | Purpose                     |
| --------------- | -------- | --------------------------- |
| `locale`        | 365 days | Language (`en` / `nl`)      |
| `appearance`    | 365 days | Light / dark / system theme |
| `sidebar_state` | 7 days   | Staff sidebar open/closed   |

Matching values may also be stored in `localStorage`.

## Consent

Because we only use necessary cookies and first-party preference cookies (no third-party trackers), we do **not** show a blocking cookie banner at this time. If we introduce analytics or advertising technologies, we will ask for consent first and update this statement.

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies will prevent login and core features from working.
