## 4. AI features

Some features may use machine-learning models (for example transcription or document assistance). Where enabled by the firm:

- You or your firm are informed in-product that AI is used
- Content may be sent to sub-processors that host models — see the customer DPA sub-processor list
- Outputs can be inaccurate; humans remain responsible for legal and professional decisions
- We do not use customer content to train public foundation models unless a written agreement expressly says so (**confirm with counsel / vendor contracts**)

## 5. Recipients and sub-processors

We share data with:

- The customer firm (controller) and its authorised users
- Infrastructure and service sub-processors (hosting, storage, email, AI, monitoring) listed in the customer DPA
- Professional advisers or authorities where legally required

## 7. Retention

We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes above, customer contract, and legal duties. Product defaults (configurable) include approximately:

- Soft-deleted clients/dossiers: about **1095 days** before permanent purge
- Client portal grants: about **90 days** after expiry/revocation
- Data-subject request handling: about **30 days** to respond; export archives about **72 hours** download window

Firms may have longer professional retention duties; erasure of client matter data can be limited by those duties (GDPR Art. 17(3)).

## 8. Your rights

Subject to GDPR conditions, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and portability. Firms can use in-product data-subject request tools for client data they control. You may lodge a complaint with the **Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens** (Netherlands) or your local supervisory authority.

To exercise rights against Qompose as controller, contact **[privacy@example.com]**. For client/matter data, contact the firm that invited you.
